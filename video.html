Video takes minutes, not seconds.

Submitting starts a job on the provider's side and hands back a task ID. This page polls it for you until the video is ready. Leave the tab open — or note the ID and come back.

Animating a still? Upload it at imghippo.com and paste the direct link. It has to be publicly reachable — the provider fetches the image from its own servers, not from your phone.

Doing it yourself

Two calls: submit, then poll until the status is succeeded.

# submit — returns a task id
curl "…/v1/videos/generated" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer of_live_your_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"wan2.7-i2v","prompt":"a paper boat","image":"https://…/still.jpg"}'

# poll — same id, until status is succeeded
curl "…/v1/videos/tasks/TASK_ID?model=wan2.7-i2v" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer of_live_your_key"
